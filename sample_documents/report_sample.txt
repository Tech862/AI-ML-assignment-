QUARTERLY REPORT

Executive Summary: Customer support response times improved during the third quarter of 2026.
The operations team reviewed 2,400 service requests between July 1, 2026 and September 30, 2026.
Findings: The average first response time fell from 12 hours to 7 hours after the team introduced a shared triage process.
Conclusion: The team recommends continuing the triage process and reviewing customer satisfaction in January 2027.
